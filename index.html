Schlieren imaging toolkit

The code behind my Schlieren research and the STREAMS fan array

A full pipeline for seeing airflow: capture a background, measure how a density gradient displaces it, and turn that displacement into an image of the flow. It runs on a webcam outdoors as well as in the lab.

  • Background-oriented SchlierenOpenCV processing of live webcam video, with Kalman-filter tracking and wavelength-coded color backgrounds.
  • MATLAB simulationsLens optics and air-density gradient models used to design the imaging setup.
  • STREAMS fan arrayA programmable, modular wind-tunnel fan array driven by Arduino PWM, with a 3D control GUI. Filed as a provisional patent.
  • Live feedA Flask site and an Android app that stream the processed Schlieren video.

  • OpenCV

  • Arduino
  • Flask

About

A first-year who likes to see how things work.

I'm studying physics at Princeton, with minors in materials science and in optimization and quantitative decision science. Most of my work comes down to one loop: build a model of something messy, measure it, and find out where the model is wrong.

Outside the lab, I sing with Tarana, play lead guitar in an Indo-rock band, co-founded a catering business that supports families in Kolkata, and spent four years teaching English to refugees.
